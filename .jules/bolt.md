## 2024-11-03 - [Avoid `.get()` on signals with large data structures in Leptos]
**Learning:** In Leptos, calling `.get()` on a signal containing a large struct (like `Slide`, which has a large string `svg_data` and multiple `Vec`s) clones the entire struct. In several reactive closures (e.g. `Memo::new`), this was happening repeatedly on every dependency change. For `Memo<Option<T>>`, correctly destructuring by reference requires `.with(|opt| opt.as_ref().map(|inner| ...))`.
**Action:** Always prefer `.with()` over `.get()` when reading from signals holding large structures. When working with `Option<T>` inside the signal, ensure to use `.as_ref()` before `.map()` to prevent cloning the `Option` contents if not explicitly needed, or only clone the specific inner fields needed (like `inner.svg_data.clone()`) rather than the entire struct.
